# Showaria Entertainment Alignment & MVP Product Strategy

Showaria is an enter-tech company specializing in **Virtual Entertainment driven by AI and XR technology**, creating immersive narratives such as *Moonbona Showcase*, *Apartment: Ripley's World*, and *The Great Judgment* [1]. To impress Showaria’s leadership with a high-impact demo, **Persona Room** must transcend standard chat utility and operate as an **interactive virtual-artist broadcast** where fans actively co-direct the performer's reality.

## Strategic Pillars for the Showaria Pitch

| Pillar | Showaria Principle | Persona Room Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Virtual Artist Persona** | Characters with deep lore, emotional depth, and presence (e.g., Moonbona from 2080) | Rina and Joon as live digital companions with mood states, proactive nudges, and memory recall. |
| **Interactive Storytelling** | Audience participation that changes narrative direction and unlocks outcomes | Real-time audience voting (`/room/[id]`) that forces the performer to react and adapt in-character. |
| **Live Broadcast Immersion** | High-energy visual feedback, synchronized voice, and shared room presence | Edge Neural streaming voice, progressive audio, dynamic mood atmosphere, and synchronized reactions. |

## Proposed Feature Roadmap for High-Impact Demo

1. **Synchronized Room Energy Feed**: Bridge public room reactions (`♥`, `🔥`, `😂`) directly into the private chat experience so individual users feel the collective pulse of the audience while talking to Rina.
2. **Interactive Stage Gamification**: Introduce "Fan Milestones" where accumulating room votes unlocks exclusive avatar expressions and special dialogue branches.
3. **Theatrical Show Mode**: An opening broadcast trailer sequence that frames the session as an active 2080 virtual stream rather than a blank webpage.
